The gate that evolves

cleargate evolve is a policy fuzzer with no LLM in it. It mutates every attack the policy stopped and keeps whatever gets through.

It found a hole in our own rule. We compared the branch name as a raw string, and generation 2 got five writes to production past it: MAIN, Main, heads/main, refs/heads/main, refs/heads/master. We now compare the normalised ref. The re-run found 0 gaps in 585 cases over 6 generations, and the real task still passed. Seed 7.
